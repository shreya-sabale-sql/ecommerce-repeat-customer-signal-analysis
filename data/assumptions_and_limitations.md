This project uses product-level behavioral proxies to infer repeat customer signals in the absence of direct customer identifiers.

Assumptions:
- Month-over-month unit growth reflects demand continuity
- Stable pricing with declining discounts indicates organic demand
- Rising discounts required to sustain volume indicate incentive-driven demand

Limitations:
- No direct CAC or marketing spend data available
- No customer cohort or lifetime value modeling
- Results are directional and intended for strategic insight
